# US Franchise FDD Disclosure Statistics — 3082 Brands (2026)

Per-brand headline facts from officially registered US Franchise Disclosure Documents (FDDs) for 3082 franchise brands: total initial investment range (Item 7), initial franchise fee (Item 5), royalty (Item 6), whether the franchisor discloses earnings (Item 19, 1671/3082 do) with the headline average unit revenue where disclosed, franchised outlet counts and closures/terminations (Item 20), and franchisee-initiated lawsuit counts (Item 3). Sourced from the Minnesota CARDS and Wisconsin DFI franchise registries.

See [`us-franchise-fdd-disclosure-statistics.csv`](./us-franchise-fdd-disclosure-statistics.csv). Column notes: blank = the FDD does not disclose it or the
table did not parse cleanly — values are never estimated. `item19_headline_avg_revenue_usd`
is the headline average-unit-revenue figure the franchisor itself represents; Item 19
representations vary in basis (gross sales vs net, subsets of units) — read the FDD for the fine print.

Compiled and maintained by **[Franchise Facts Report](https://franchisefactsreport.com)** (franchisefactsreport.com) — free per-brand
FDD fact pages, [category rankings](https://franchisefactsreport.com/rankings/) (cheapest, highest disclosed revenue,
lowest churn, most lawsuits), and [head-to-head comparisons](https://franchisefactsreport.com/vs/). Licensed CC-BY-4.0.
Headline figures only; full structured reports (every fee line, complete Item 19, litigation detail,
peer benchmarks) at [franchisefactsreport.com](https://franchisefactsreport.com).
